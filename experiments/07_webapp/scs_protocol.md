# SCS Evaluation Protocol

Pre-registered protocol for the human evaluation of the EWS prototype.

## 1. Objective
To assess whether the SHAP-based explanations generated by the EWS are
comprehensible and actionable for non-technical academic staff, using the
System Causability Scale (SCS; Holzinger et al., 2020).

## 2. Participants
- Target: 3 to 6 academic coordinators or advisors.
- Inclusion criteria: Familiar with student monitoring at the university level.

## 3. Tasks
The participant will be asked to complete three tasks using the prototype:
1. **Triage:** Identify the top 5 most at-risk students in the current window
   (W8).
2. **Interpretation:** Open the risk card for a specific student and explain
   *why* the system flagged them (identify the top risk factor).
3. **Action:** Propose a specific intervention based on the risk card.

## 4. Instrument
The SCS (10 items, Likert 1-5) is administered immediately after the tasks.
The questions are integrated into the web interface (`/scs` route).

## 5. Scoring and Analysis
- The SCS total score is calculated as `sum(scores) / 50` (range 0.0 - 1.0).
- Given the small sample size (n=3-6), results will be reported descriptively
  (mean, median, individual scores) and supplemented with qualitative feedback.
- The hypothesis is that the average SCS score will be >= 0.7, indicating
  acceptable actionability.

## 6. Qualitative Feedback
During the session, the evaluator will take notes on:
- Which factors the coordinators found most useful.
- Any confusion regarding log-odds vs. probability, or feature names.
- Suggestions for the final thesis discussion on operational deployment.
