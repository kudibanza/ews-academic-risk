### Frozen scientific decisions
1. Target primary: Withdrawn ∪ Fail vs {Pass, Distinction}.
   Secondary (EXPLORATORY only): Withdrawn.
2. Windows: W2=14, W4=28, W6=42, W8=56 (days since course start).
3. Exclusion: drop at window k iff date_unregistration.notna() AND
   date_unregistration <= cutoff_k. NEVER depend on date_registration.
4. Zero-leakage Rules R1–R4:
   R1 preprocessing isolation (fit only on train fold);
   R2 resampling only train fold after split;
   R3 temporal boundary (features use date <= cutoff, computed before split);
   R4 GroupKFold by student_id. R3+R4 jointly.
5. Validation: GroupKFold(5) by student_id; cross-cohort train
   {2013B,2013J} -> test {2014B,2014J}, no retraining, no threshold recalib.
6. Imbalance: none/SMOTE/ADASYN/Tomek/class_weight; calibrate Platt or
   isotonic; report ECE, Brier.
7. Cost: C_FN:C_FP = 5:1 primary; 3:1 and 10:1 sensitivity. theta in
   [0.1,0.9] step 0.01, minimize expected cost on validation fold.
8. Models: LogReg, DecisionTree; RF, XGBoost, LightGBM.
9. Metrics: PR-AUC, Recall, F2 (primary); Precision, ROC-AUC, ECE, Brier,
   expected_cost (secondary). Seed 42.
10. §14 output: JSONL, one record per (student_id, window, presentation)
    with risk_score, threshold_applied, flagged, top_positive_factors,
    top_negative_factors, base_value; stored results/shap_artifacts/.
11. §5 features (7): S_vle, D_active, H_entropy, Delta_t, S_score,
    still_registered, click_trend.
12. §16 benchmark variants (FROZEN):
    - W2 Variant A: retain Delta_t and S_score with training-fold imputation.
    - W2 Variant B: drop Delta_t and S_score in W2 only.
    - RESULT: A marginally better (+0.003 to +0.011 PR-AUC). A retained.

## Benchmark results (summary)

| Window | Best model | PR-AUC | ROC-AUC | Recall @0.5 | Precision | F2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W2 | lightgbm (A) | 0.619 ± 0.005 | 0.671 | 0.520 | 0.608 | 0.535 |
| W4 | lightgbm | 0.694 ± 0.011 | 0.737 | 0.568 | 0.668 | 0.585 |
| W6 | lightgbm | 0.715 ± 0.012 | 0.762 | 0.577 | 0.689 | 0.596 |
| W8 | lightgbm | 0.745 ± 0.019 | 0.788 | 0.580 | 0.717 | 0.603 |

Findings:
- Model ranking stable across windows: lightgbm > xgboost > rf > logreg > dtree.
- Temporal monotonic improvement: 0.619 → 0.694 → 0.715 → 0.745 PR-AUC.
  Largest gain W2→W4 (+0.075), coinciding with assessment coverage jump.
- W2 A vs B: A marginally better (+0.003 to +0.011 PR-AUC). A retained.
- Recall at threshold 0.5 is ~0.58 in W8 → 42% of at-risk students missed
  at default cutoff. This is the Step 4 baseline.
- Fold stability: pos_rate_test varies by at most 3.4 pp (W8);
  per-fold PR-AUC std 0.004–0.023. GroupKFold produces balanced folds.
