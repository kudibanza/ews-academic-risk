   Secondary (EXPLORATORY only): Withdrawn.

   date_unregistration <= cutoff_k. NEVER depend on date_registration.

   R1 preprocessing isolation; R2 resampling only train fold;
   R3 temporal boundary; R4 GroupKFold by student_id. R3+R4 jointly.

   students removed from test. COMPLETED.

   ADASYN failed in W2 (documented, refs [57][58]).

8. Selected model: LightGBM + class_weight, uncalibrated.
   Calibration tested in 4.2 and REJECTED (LightGBM already calibrated,
